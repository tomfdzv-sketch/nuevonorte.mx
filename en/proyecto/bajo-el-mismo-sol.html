Bajo el Mismo Sol

2026 · Galicia, SpainDocumentary · In post-productionDirected by: Tomás Fernández Vértiz

An observational documentary shot in Galicia over five trips. A single imagined day — dawn to night — built from the lives of people who do not know each other: stonemasons, tamborileiros, beekeepers, the entroido. Currently in post-production.
